2. Information Collected from Partners and How It Is Used

The ParkCo Partner application collects and processes only the information necessary to enable hosts to list parking inventory, coordinate vehicle check-in/check-out, and receive earnings:

  • Host Identification & Contact Details: Name, phone number, and email address provided during registration. Used to authenticate host sessions, verify property management rights, and transmit booking confirmations and gate-pass PIN verifications.
  • Precise Parking Facility Location: When adding or modifying a parking space listing, the app requests device location permissions (foreground only) to pinpoint the exact GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) of the parking bay or driveway entrance so drivers can accurately navigate to the spot. GPS location is never collected in the background or shared for ad-tracking.
  • Parking Facility Images (Camera & Storage): Camera and photo access is requested when a host captures pictures of their parking spot, entrance barriers, security signage, or EV charging ports. These photos are displayed to drivers on the customer app to identify the parking area. We do not inspect or upload personal photos outside of the images selected by the host.
  • Banking and Financial Details for Payouts: To receive automated earnings settlements for completed parking reservations, hosts submit bank account details (account number, IFSC code, or UPI VPA). All financial data is tokenized and transmitted directly to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payout processors (such as Razorpay Route). We do not store unencrypted financial credentials on our general application servers.
  • Device and Performance Telemetry: Device hardware model, Android OS version, and crash diagnostic logs to maintain application reliability, prevent fraud, and guarantee operational uptime.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or lease host personal or financial data to data brokers or advertising networks. We disclose information solely to the following trusted third parties strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment & Settlement Gateway: Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant) for disbursing host parking payouts.
  • Mapping Services: Google Maps Platform for displaying map views and rendering property locations.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud hosting facilities with modern TLS/HTTPS encrypted transport.

4. Data Security and Storage

All communication between the ParkCo Partner application and our backend endpoints is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3). We enforce strict role-based access control to prevent unauthorized modification or exposure of host listings or financial settlement data.
