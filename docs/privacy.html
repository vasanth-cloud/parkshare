2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect and process the following categories of data strictly to provide parking discovery, reservation, and space hosting services:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, and phone number provided during account registration to authenticate users, facilitate booking confirmations, and issue digital parking gate passes.
  • Precise and Approximate Location Data: When permitted by the user, we access device GPS location in the foreground to locate nearby available parking spots, compute driving distance, and display navigation routes to selected parking facilities. Location data is never sold or used for background advertising tracking.
  • Photos and Device Camera: Camera access is requested solely when a user takes a photo of their parking spot, vehicle, or odometer to verify space quality or complete booking check-in/check-out. We do not access your general photo library without your explicit selection.
  • Financial and Payment Data: All payments for parking spaces and services are securely tokenized and processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We never collect, process, or store raw credit/debit card numbers or CVVs on our servers.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique crash identifiers to diagnose technical errors and ensure application stability.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal identification information to third parties. We disclose data solely to trusted third-party providers who assist us in operating our platform under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant) for processing financial transactions.
  • Mapping Services: Google Maps Platform for displaying map tiles and calculating directions.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud servers with TLS/HTTPS encrypted endpoints.

4. Data Retention and Security

All sensitive information exchanged between the app and our backend servers is transmitted over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections. We maintain industry-standard physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
